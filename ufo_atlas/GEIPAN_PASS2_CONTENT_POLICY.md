# Watchers Project UFO Atlas — GEIPAN Pass 2 Content Policy

Effective: 2026-09-06

## Public encounter classification

All customer-facing GEIPAN case records use the Atlas encounter-class field with only these values:

- **CE1** — observational encounter: reported craft/object/phenomenon without a stronger CE2–CE5 feature.
- **CE2** — reported physical interaction/effect associated with the encounter, including physical traces, vehicle/electrical effects, physiological effects, or animal/environmental effects.
- **CE3** — reported being/entity/occupant associated with the encounter or craft.
- **CE4** — reported abduction, involuntary transport, boarding/taken experience, or equivalent direct removal/transport by the phenomenon.
- **CE5** — reported human-initiated contact or signaling that is described as receiving a response from the phenomenon/entity.

When more than one class applies to one case, the public case class is the highest applicable encounter level in this order: CE5, CE4, CE3, CE2, CE1. Lower-level features remain available in the case narrative and source/provenance data.

The encounter class describes the **reported event content**. It does not assert that the report is true, extraterrestrial, unexplained, or accepted by GEIPAN. GEIPAN's source classification (A/B/C/D and source conclusion) is retained separately and must not be converted into the Atlas CE field.

## Language policy

The UFO Atlas is English-leaning.

- The principal case narrative is presented **English first**.
- The original French case narrative is preserved immediately afterward as the source-language text.
- Normalized customer-facing metadata is English wherever translation/localization is appropriate.
- Proper names, official place names, case identifiers, URLs, dates, and other non-translatable identifiers are retained accurately rather than artificially Anglicized.
- Original French metadata/source values remain preserved in provenance/raw-source storage even when the normalized Atlas field is English.
- Translation may improve grammar and readability, but must not invent facts, intensify claims, or remove uncertainty expressed by the source.

## Preservation rule

Pass 2 is additive/normalizing. The original GEIPAN source records, source wording, source classification, source URLs, and source-language narrative are not discarded or overwritten by the English normalization layer.
